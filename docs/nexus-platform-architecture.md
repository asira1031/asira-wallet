# Nexus Platform — B2B infrastructure boundary

Nexus Platform is a separate, B2B-only prototype for licensed operators. It is not part of Pay Money and it does not accept player funds, generate wagers, or settle payouts.

## Product modules

- Tenant and brand configuration
- Approved game-provider catalog integration
- Operator onboarding and environment management
- Compliance-control configuration: KYC, AML, responsible-gaming, audit trail
- Immutable operational activity log

## Deliberate exclusions

- Player deposits, withdrawals, balances, or cash-outs
- Real-money wagering or game-result settlement
- Payment gateways and financial custody
- Production KYC/AML decisions or regulator submissions

## Commercial delivery path

1. Build and demonstrate in a sandbox.
2. Contract with a licensed operator.
3. Complete legal, security, certification, and regulator requirements for the operator's jurisdiction.
4. Integrate approved games and operator-controlled payments only after approvals.
